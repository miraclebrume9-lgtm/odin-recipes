Noodles and Egg

Image credit: MK Onota

Description

Noodles and Egg with Fried Plantain is a quick, comforting meal that combines savory, seasoned noodles, a perfectly fried egg, and sweet, golden slices of friend plantain.

Ingredients

  • 2 packs of instant noodles (with seasoning packets)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 ripe plantain, sliced into rounds or diagonal
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil (for frying plantain and egg)
  • Half a medium onion, sliced
  • Fresh pepper and chopped vegetables (optional)

Cooking Steps

  1. Fry the plantain slices in hot vegetable oil over medium heat until both sides turn a beautiful golden brown, then drain them on pepper towels.
  2. Boil the noodle cakes in two cups of water for about 3 minutes until they begin to soften up and loosen.
  3. Stir in the noodle seasonings, siiced onions, and chopped fresh peppers, letting the liquid simmer down until the noodles are tender and the sauce thickens.
  4. Fry your egg in a seperate slightly greased pan - you can scramble it or make a perfect sunny-side-up or fried egg depending on what you prefer.
  5. Assemble your plate by placing the seasoned noodles at the center, then laying your fried egg and sweet plantain slices neatly on top.
