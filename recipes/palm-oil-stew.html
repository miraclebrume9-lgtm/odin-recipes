Palm Oil Stew

photocredit @cookingwithclaudy

Description

Palm Oil Stew, also known as Ayamase or Ofada Stew, is a uniquely flavorful Nigerian delicacy made with a roughly blended green pepper mix, bleached palm oil, and a rich combination of assorted meats or protein of choice.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of palm oil
  • 5 large green bell peppers (or habanero peppers)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • Assorted meats (boiled beef, tripe/shaki, or chicken)
  • 1 cup of boiled eggs
  • 2 tablespoon of ground crayfish
  • 2 seasoning cubes and salt to taste

Cooking Steps

  1. Bleach the palm oil in a covered pot on medium heat for about 10 minutes until it turns amber-clear, then let it cool down slightly.
  2. Sauté the chopped onions in the bleached oil over medium-high heat until they become translucent and fragrant.
  3. Pour in the blended pepper mix (which you rough blended and drain beforehand) and fry it down until the oil begins to float to the top.
  4. Add the assorted proteins along with the meat stock, ground crayfish, seasoning cubes, and a pinch of salt.
  5. Simmer the mixture on medium heat for 15 minutes so the proteins soak up all the rich pepper and oil flavors.
  6. Gently drop in the boiled eggs during the final 5 minutes of cooking, letting everything fuse together before turning off the heat.
