Egusi Soup

photo credit @joyfulcook

Description

Egusi soup is a rich, savory West African soup made from ground melon seeds, leafy vegetables, and delicious local spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of ground egusi seeds(melon seeds)
  • 1 cup of palm oil
  • Assorted meats (beef, shaki, cow skin)
  • 2 tablespoon of ground crayfish
  • 1 cup of chopped pumpkin leaves(ugo) or bitterleaf
  • 2 seasoning cubes and salt to taste

Steps

  1. Boil your assorted meats and smoked fish with sliced onions, seasoning cubes, and salt until tender. Save the rich meat stock (broth) in the pot.
  2. Mix the ground egusi seeds with a small amount of warm water in a bowl to form a thick, smooth paste.
  3. Heat the palm oil in a seperate clean pot on medium heat for 2 minutes (do not bleach it), then add sliced onions and your egusi paste clumps to fry.
  4. Stir the frying egusi gently for about 10 minutes until it forms small, firm yellow cakes and releases a pleasant aroma.
  5. Pour in the meat stock along with your cooked meats, ground crayfish, and pepper. Stir well, cover the pot, and let it simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. Add the chopped vegetables (ugwu or bitterleaf), stir gently to combine, and leave the pot open to simmer for a final 5 minutes before turning off the heat.
